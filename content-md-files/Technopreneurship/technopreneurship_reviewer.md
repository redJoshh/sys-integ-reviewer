### Definition: Technology

**1. The word 'technology' comes from which two Greek words?**

- B. Word or utterance by which thought is expressed
- C. Study of machines
- D. Science of profit

> **Explanation:** Techne = art, skill, craft, or the way, manner, or means by which a thing is gained.

- A. Art or craft
- B. Tool or machine
